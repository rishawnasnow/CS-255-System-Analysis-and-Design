# CS-255-System-Analysis-and-Design
This repository contains my CS-255 Systems Analysis and Design projects, focusing on the DriverPass system. It includes business requirements, UML diagrams, plus system design documentation showing how customer needs can be turned into a functional software design.

# CS-255 Systems Analysis and Design

## DriverPass Project Reflection

### 1. Briefly summarize the DriverPass project.
DriverPass was a company looking to create an online system to help customers prepare for their driving tests. Customers needed a way to register, purchase training packages, take practice tests, or schedule driving lessons. Employees also needed access to manage appointments, update customer information, or review business reports. Main focus was creating a system that would make daily operations easier while meeting customer needs.

### 2. What did you do particularly well?
Organization was one area that helped me throughout this project. From my previous experience in banking, paying attention to details was always important, especially when handling financial transactions or customer information. Those same skills helped when reviewing requirements or putting together UML diagrams. Breaking assignments into smaller steps made it easier to understand how different parts of DriverPass connected.

### 3. What would you improve?
Looking back, my UML diagrams would be one area worth improving. Learning how to use different shapes, connections, or symbols took some practice. Some parts were more challenging than expected, especially making sure everything was organized correctly. With more experience, those diagrams could include clearer connections or additional details to make them easier for someone else to follow.

### 4. How did you consider user needs in your design?
Understanding what customers or employees needed was important before designing DriverPass. Customers wanted a convenient way to schedule lessons or prepare for driving tests. Employees needed tools to manage appointments or keep track of information. Those needs helped determine which features to include. In my current job, using different systems has shown me how important it is for technology to support daily responsibilities. When systems aren't designed around users, even simple tasks can become complicated.

### 5. How do you approach designing software? What will you use in the future?
One thing this course taught me was how much planning goes into software development before coding even begins. Understanding what needs to be accomplished, asking questions, plus keeping information organized are important parts of my approach. Creating UML diagrams helped me see how different parts of a system work together. Moving forward, reviewing requirements, communicating clearly, or breaking larger problems into smaller steps will be useful. As a computer science student working toward a career in IT, these are skills that will help me in future courses or professional opportunities.
